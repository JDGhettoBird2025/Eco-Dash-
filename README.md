# Eco Dash - African Logistics

## Project Description

Eco Dash is a 2D web-based game that was developed through the use of HTML5 Canvas, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript.

The game gives players control over a delivery drone responsible for completing a delivery mission while also being able to manage limited battery power and navigating environmental obstacles.

The game is inspired by African logistics and infrastructure challenges, including the need to transport supplies across areas where roads and infrastructure may be difficult to access.

The game also incorporates renewable energy through the use of a solar energy charging unit. 

## Game Objective 
The objective of Eco Dash is to navigate the drone through the environment and successfully reach the delivery point while managing the drone's battery.

The player must:

- Navigate the drone around trees and environmental obstacles.
- Cross the river using the bridge.
- Manage battery consumption.
- Recharge the drone at the solar station.
- Navigate against changing wind conditions.
- Complete the delivery mission.
- Earn points for completing the mission.
